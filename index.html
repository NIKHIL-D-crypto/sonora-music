Playback

Lock screen & headsetSONORA uses the Media Session API where your browser supports it: play, pause, previous, next, seeking and 10-second skip. Which buttons appear depends on your Android version and browser.
Dolby Atmos & sound effectsSONORA plays your files untouched so playback stays stable in the background. For Dolby Atmos or equaliser effects, use your phone’s sound settings; they usually apply to every app.
